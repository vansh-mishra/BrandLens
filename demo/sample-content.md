# BrandLens demo content

## Sample that should trigger findings

LUMALEAF'S MIRACLE SERUM IS 100% SUSTAINABLE!!! It is clinically proven to cure acne fast. Our chemical-free formula delivers guaranteed results.

Expected themes: absolute environmental claim, medical/clinical claim, unapproved terminology, guarantee language, and overly promotional tone.

## Sample that should mostly pass the configured rules

Build a straightforward skincare routine with LumaLeaf. Calm Cleanser contains 2% niacinamide. Our refill pouch uses 70% less packaging plastic by weight than the standard 250 ml bottle.

Expected themes: approved facts can be stated with their intended scope. This is still not a certification of truth or legal compliance.
